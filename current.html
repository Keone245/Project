Real Madrid currently

The current state of the club!

As of writing this, Madrid are not in the best of forms. With only 8 matches left to play in the season, they are 7 points behind league-leaders Barcelona, and are 2-1 down on aggregate against Bayern Munich in the UCL. To add to this, they have already sacked Xabi Alonso, and are at risk of doing the same for Alvaro Arbeloa if they don't improve. Though the chances of winning La Liga are slim, they still believe they can come back against Bayern Munich.
