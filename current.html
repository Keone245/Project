Real Madrid currently

Now, let's talk about the current state of Los Blancos!

As of writing this, Real Madrid are not in the best of forms. They currently sit 4 points behind Barca, 2nd in the table. Despite hiring Xabi Alonso and Trent Alexander Arnold, we still struggle to find the rythm we had 2 seasons ago.
However, this is not yet the end for Madrid, since there are still plenty of matches to come this season, and more opportunities to bounce back.
To add to this, Xabi is at risk of getting sacked, but he and the players are tyring their best to prevent this from happening.
