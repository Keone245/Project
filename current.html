Real Madrid currently

The current state of Los Blancos!

As of writing this, Real Madrid are not in the best of form. They currently sit 4 points behind Barça, 2nd in the table. Despite hiring Xabi Alonso and Trent Alexander-Arnold, we still struggle to find the rhythm we had 2 seasons ago.
However, this is not yet the end for Madrid; there are still plenty of matches to come this season and more opportunities to bounce back.
To add to this, Xabi is at risk of getting sacked, but he and the players are trying their best to prevent this from happening.
