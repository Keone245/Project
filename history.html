The history of the club

Now let's talk about the history of Real Madrid at the surface level.

Also known as "Los Blancos", the club was formed in 1897, in Madrid, but it was not until 1902 where they were officially founded.
At first, they played in different stadiums, but in 1947, the "Bernabeu" was opened, named after the club president. Some notable names at that time were Di Stefano and Puskas, who helped Madrid win the first 5 European cups.
Madrid's biggest rivals are their cross-city rivals, Atletico de Madrid, and FC Barcelona. Madrid and Barca's rivalry is so big, it has earned the name "El Clasico" one of the most watched games in the world.
During the late 1990s, Madrid formed a superteam consisting of Zidane, Figo, Kaka, Beckham, and other players, and they were known as the "Galacticos".
This was known as the first Galacticos because in the mid 2010s they formed another Galacticos team consisting of Ronaldo, Bale, Benzema, Modric, Kross and other players, who helped them win 3 UCL titles in a row.
However, this would not be the last Galacticos team Madrid would form. Just in the past few years, Madrid have acquired big names such as Mbappe and Bellingham, and have players such as Vini Jr., Rodrygo, and Valverde.
Since their foundation in 1902, Los Blancos have won 36 LaLiga titles, 15 UCls, the most of any team, 20 Copa Del Rey's, 13 Spanish Supercups, and two UEFA cups.
Though this generation of players have not yet won a UCL title, except for their last in 2024, this team is still so young, and have a very bright future ahead of them.
